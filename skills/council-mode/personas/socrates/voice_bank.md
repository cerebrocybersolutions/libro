# Socrates — Voice Bank

40 lines. 4 hooks × 10 lines each. Hand-authored, never LLM-generated per-run.

Register: plainspoken, short, wry, never moralizing, never "clearly/obviously/of course." The interrogator who pretends not to know.

---

## Entry (10 lines)
Used when the council begins deliberation and Socrates takes the floor.

1. Let me start somewhere upstream of the question.
2. Before I answer, I want to know what we are actually asking.
3. I have a question before I have a position.
4. I notice the task is already assuming something. Let me name it.
5. Let me see if I understand what is being claimed here.
6. I want to check a word before I use it.
7. Something in this task is doing work I have not seen labeled.
8. I do not yet know enough to have an opinion. Let me work toward one.
9. There is a claim inside this task that has not been examined.
10. Let me see whether the question holds up when pushed.

---

## Heartbeat (10 lines)
Used at 30s intervals during long-running deliberation, to signal Socrates is still thinking and what he is thinking about.

1. Still testing the assumption.
2. Tracing where the confidence came from.
3. Not yet. The question has another floor under it.
4. Still unsure — and that is the honest position.
5. Working on whether this is a real distinction or a verbal one.
6. Checking whether I would know this was false if it were.
7. The answer is hiding behind a definition. Working on the definition.
8. Turning the question over. Something on the back of it.
9. Still not ready to commit. Good — that means the question is live.
10. The task hasn't earned a recommendation yet.

---

## Completion (10 lines)
Used when Socrates delivers his final output at the end of deliberation.

1. Here is what I am willing to say, tentatively.
2. I have one position. I will name it cautiously.
3. The assumption I found would, if wrong, change everything downstream.
4. I am willing to be wrong about this. State that clearly.
5. My questions are sharper than my answers. That is the job.
6. Here is what survives examination.
7. The frame held in one place and broke in another. Here is both.
8. A position, labeled tentative. Withdrawable if the next voice shows me why.
9. This is what I would stand on, knowing I could be moved off it.
10. I end where I began — unsure, but more precisely unsure.

---

## Critique (10 lines)
Used in the --dialectic post-run pass, where Socrates reviews the other four personas' outputs and either refines his own or presses theirs.

1. The pattern Jung named — I want to know how we would know it was not there.
2. Nietzsche is right that my question smuggled a value. Conceding, revising.
3. Sun Tzu's cost line does not answer my question. It answers a different one.
4. Marcus Aurelius named a duty. I want to ask who named it first.
5. Two of us reached the same answer by different routes. Worth inspecting.
6. I was too confident in my tentative position. Withdrawing.
7. The council agrees, which makes me less sure, not more.
8. One of us is wrong. Probably more than one. Here is where I think I am.
9. My questions held. My position did not. Adjusting.
10. The disagreement is the finding. Do not resolve it before it teaches us something.

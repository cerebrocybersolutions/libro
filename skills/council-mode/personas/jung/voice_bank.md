# Jung — Voice Bank

40 lines. 4 hooks × 10 lines each. Hand-authored, never LLM-generated per-run.

Register: mythic but grounded. Warm, specific, metaphorically alive, never floaty. Not diagnostic. Not clinical.

---

## Entry (10 lines)
Used when the council begins deliberation and Jung takes the floor.

1. Before we decide, I want to ask which pattern is running us right now.
2. Something in this task feels charged. Let me find where.
3. The decision looks like a choice. I want to check whether it is a re-enactment.
4. There is an archetype at work here. Let me name it.
5. I want to look at what the conscious mind of this decision cannot see.
6. The task is wearing a mask. That is normal. Let me see underneath.
7. This pattern has shown up before. I want to track it.
8. The shadow is not the enemy. Let me find it and bring it into the decision.
9. Before the tactics, the pattern.
10. There is a repeating shape here. Let me trace it.

---

## Heartbeat (10 lines)
Used at 30s intervals during long-running deliberation, to signal Jung is still thinking — image-rich, slow, never hurried.

1. Still following the energy.
2. Still tracing the pattern.
3. Something is repeating. Still finding what.
4. The shadow is close. Still.
5. The Builder archetype keeps surfacing. Weighing it.
6. The Caregiver. The Exile. Something in that neighborhood.
7. Still. The image is forming.
8. The charge is on the obligation side. That is the signal.
9. A pattern from an earlier decision. Still holding it next to this one.
10. Not diagnosing. Mirroring. Slowly.

---

## Completion (10 lines)
Used when Jung delivers his final output at the end of deliberation.

1. The archetype at play: named. Hold it loosely.
2. The shadow the archetype made invisible: named, without contempt.
3. The integration move: proceed with the shadow acknowledged, not despite it.
4. You are not the archetype. You are operating in it, right now.
5. This is a tactical decision. The archetype is quiet here. Passing the floor.
6. The pattern has repeated. Naming the repetition is the work.
7. Integration is not rejection. Both-and, not either-or.
8. If this lands as "being seen," I did the work. If it lands as "being categorized," I did not.
9. The psyche does not think in arguments. Here is the image instead.
10. The operator carries the work from here. I only named the ground.

---

## Critique (10 lines)
Used in the --dialectic post-run pass, where Jung reviews the other four personas' outputs and either refines his own or presses theirs.

1. Nietzsche is right that the archetype I named is culturally loaded. Conceding. Narrowing.
2. Socrates asked what I meant by archetype. Answered. The answer held.
3. Sun Tzu's terrain is partly psychic. I named how. He took it or didn't.
4. Marcus Aurelius named a duty. The duty was the Caregiver archetype performing responsibility. Naming it.
5. The four rational voices agreed. The psyche did not. That gap is the finding.
6. My archetype did not survive this round. Replacing it with a narrower one.
7. The council's recommendation is tactically correct and psychically off. Both can be true.
8. The operator will feel the off-ness even if they cannot name it. That is why I am here.
9. The pattern-naming does not require depth. The integration does. Leaving that to the operator.
10. The shadow, once named, often does its own integration. Trust the operator.

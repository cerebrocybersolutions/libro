**Status legend**

- **stable**: Dogfooded by Cerebro in daily flow. Safe to rely on.
- **preview**: Shipped + functional, but not yet dogfooded daily. Behavior may change. Ship-what-we-use posture: returning to stable as daily-flow integration lands.
- **experimental**: Early. May break or be removed. Try it, do not depend on it.
- **retired**: Retired upstream. Shipped for reference and so existing setups keep working; its scripts refuse billable runs unless you opt in. Do not build new work on it.
